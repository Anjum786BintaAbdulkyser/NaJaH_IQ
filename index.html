About

Built around one founder's field experience — and a growing network of professionals.

Anjum Binta Abdul Kyser Founder, NaJaH_IQ

Anjum is an Islamic finance and data professional with experience across Islamic finance research, audit, data analytics, Islamic banking research, halal investment research, business consulting, product research, market analysis, branding, and digital content.

Her work includes experience with S&P Global, Amaanah Financial Services & Advisory, AlHuda CIBE (Islamic Microfinance research), and Peertechz Publications, alongside remote projects with teams in Melbourne, Australia, and independent research and consulting work in Islamic finance and the halal economy.

She started NaJaH_IQ to support the growing halal economy worldwide with practical, professional, and affordable consulting, research, analytics, branding, product, and growth services — and to create opportunities for professionals who want to build meaningful careers through halal and ethical work.

NaJaH_IQ is a professional consulting, research, analytics, branding, product management, and growth strategy platform focused on Islamic finance and the halal economy.

We support Islamic banks, Islamic finance companies, halal investment companies, Islamic fintechs, asset managers, Takaful companies, halal businesses, startups, and other organisations looking to develop and grow in a Shariah-conscious and ethical way.

Our aim is to make professional expertise more accessible by providing practical and affordable services in Islamic finance research, business consulting, data analytics, product development, market research, branding, digital content, and training.

Our Mission

To serve the Islamic economy and Muslim community with affordable, practical, and professional services — connecting businesses with skilled professionals who want to work through halal, ethical, and responsible means, while respecting their professional and religious obligations.

Our Vision

To build a global professional network for the Islamic economy, connecting businesses, researchers, analysts, and consultants across countries — and to contribute to a stronger, more inclusive, professionally supported global halal economy.

Our Commitment

We believe professional success and ethical values work together. We're committed to affordability, quality, transparency, responsible work, continuous learning, and ethical earnings.
